Late-Summer Scouting: Reading Sign Before Season Opens

In the field

With opening day a few weeks out, now is the time to get eyes on fresh sign without pushing deer off their pattern. Focus on edges between bedding and feeding areas, and check trail cameras on a low-disturbance schedule.

Gear check

Re-check broadhead alignment and re-shoot groups at 20, 30, and 40 yards before switching from field points. A shift of even an inch at distance is worth tuning out now, not in October.

Quick tip

Wind direction matters more than stand height. Pick your entry and exit routes based on prevailing wind first, cover second.
